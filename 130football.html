How it works
  • Spin to get a random Delaware high school. Only schools with at least one player who fits an open roster spot will come up.
  • Draft any listed player into the roster slot they're eligible for. (Note: FBs can fill RB or the offensive FLEX spot.)
  • You get up to two respins for the entire game if you want a different school to choose from.
  • Fill all 14 roster spots to see your simulated record.
